Sources & Image Credits

Artwork provenance, links, and reuse notes

By Tyler Tinney

How images are used

The artwork itself may be in the public domain, but a particular photograph, scan, or modern reproduction still needs its own rights check. For the images already displayed on this site, the linked pages document public-domain status or a CC0 release. Other images are deliberately placeholders, not unverified downloads.

The smaller web-sized images are requested only when needed (loading="lazy"), and there are no external fonts, trackers, or commercial site-builder scripts. Decorative vector illustrations were made for this portfolio.
